### Q12. Compare the warranty requirements of both bids.
**Scope:** `all bids`  
**Answer:** The warranty requirements specified in the documents are as follows:

For student Chromebooks (both Tier 1 Small and Tier 2 Large), the minimum warranty required is one year (JA-207652_Student_and_Staff_Computing_Devices_FINAL.pdf p.4). For student and staff Windows laptops, a three-year warranty is required (Addendum_1_RFP_JA-207652_Student_and_Staff_Computing_Devices.pdf p.3).

All warranties must be official manufacturer warranties (Addendum_1_RFP_JA-207652_Student_and_Staff_Computing_Devices.pdf p.3). Warranties become effective on the date the equipment is placed into service, must be equal to or exceed those offered to the general public, and all warranty service, repairs, or replacements must be performed by the awarded vendors at no cost within five business days of the District reporting the issue. If repairs cannot be completed within five business days, a temporary replacement device must be provided (JA-207652_Student_and_Staff_Computing_Devices_FINAL.pdf p.4).

Additional break-fix repair programs can be included as additional pricing, but are not a minimum requirement (Addendum_1_RFP_JA-207652_Student_and_Staff_Computing_Devices.pdf p.3).
